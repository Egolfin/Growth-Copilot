APPROVED PLAYBOOK

The consultative growth conversation

01

Diagnose

Ask what the merchant wants to improve: visibility, new customers, larger baskets, repeat orders, or a slow daypart.

02

Match the tool

Recommend one primary solution based on the goal. Do not lead with every product.

03

Make it concrete

Use the campaign estimate, target audience, schedule, and budget to show how the plan could fit.

04

Confirm next step

Ask for a decision or a clearly defined follow-up using a consultative close.

HIGH-IMPACT DISCOVERY

Ask only what moves the conversation forward

  1. Which outcome would make the biggest difference right now?
  2. Which days or dayparts have capacity that you would like to fill?
  3. Is your priority margin, moving an item, or easing a first order?
  4. What investment level feels comfortable for a 30-day test?

Source: Merchant Growth Guide · p. 6
